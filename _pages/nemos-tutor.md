## 🗺️ A Guide to Indian Financial Markets
This coursework is a beginner's guide for retail investors in India. We strip away intimidating jargon to give a clear understanding of the rules, the players, and where your money goes. 
* **Phase 1: The Arena:** Mapping the financial ecosystem, including Primary and Secondary markets. Check out our [Guide to The Indian Financial - The Arena (Playlist)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLDvDhlO8Ad8I)
* **Phase 2: The Arsenal:** Understanding specific instruments like Fixed Income, Equities, and Mutual Funds. (Work In Progress)
* **Phase 3: The Action:** Turning theory into practice with asset allocation, portfolio management, and investor psychology. (Yet to Start)
