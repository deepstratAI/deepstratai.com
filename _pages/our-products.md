Our products are open-source initiatives designed to empower the retail investor ("Nemo") with the knowledge and tools needed to navigate the complexities of the Indian stock market.

---

## Nemo's Tutor

**Nemo's Tutor** is our dedicated education zone taking shape across our YouTube Channel and Substack Blog. It helps Nemo learn to become the "educated" human in the loop.

[Explore Nemo's Tutor](/nemos-tutor/){: .btn .btn--danger .btn--large}

## Nemo's Vector

**Nemo's Vector** is a set of tools. It is designed to be the compass for the everyday investor. It is not a get-rich-quick scheme, but a comprehensive toolkit that combines data-driven insights with the critical element of an educated "human in the loop." It's goal is to demystify complex financial landscape and turn market chaos into a navigable chart.
